Our Philosophy

Luxury Reimagined for a New Generation

Luvia Nova was born from a conviction: that the finest diamonds need not come at the cost of the earth, and that true luxury is defined by craft, intention, and meaning — not by convention.


We serve discerning buyers who seek something rare: a jewel that honours heritage while embracing the future. Every stone is selected by hand. Every setting is crafted to order. Nothing is rushed.

"A diamond is not merely a stone. It is a promise — passed between people, carried across time." — Luvia Nova

Sales Terms & No-Return Policy Signature Required

Made-to-Order & Non-Returnable
Every Luvia Nova piece is made entirely to order — your chosen stone, your chosen setting, cast and finished specifically for you. As a result, we are unable to accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations once production has commenced.

Consultation & Confirmation
All clients are encouraged to take the time they need during the consultation and stone-selection process to feel completely confident before confirming their order. If you have any uncertainty at any stage, please raise it with your consultant before giving final approval. We are committed to getting every detail right the first time.

Pricing
Diamond prices and metal costs are quoted individually at consultation, reflecting live market rates at the time of order confirmation. Setting prices shown on this site are indicative only and subject to change with prevailing gold and platinum spot prices. Your confirmed total price will be stated in writing before production commences.

Production Timeline
Stone selection typically takes 3–7 days from consultation. Setting manufacture and finishing takes a further 3–5 weeks depending on complexity. We will provide regular updates throughout and can accommodate urgent timelines where possible.

Certification
All diamonds above 1.0ct are GIA or IGI certified unless the client explicitly opts for an uncertified stone at consultation. Uncertified stones carry a reduced price but do not include a third-party grading report.

Governing Law
These terms are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario, Canada, and applicable federal Canadian consumer protection legislation. Any disputes shall be resolved in accordance with those laws.

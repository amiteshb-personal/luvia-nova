# Luvia Nova — Project Brief

## Brand
**Luvia Nova** is a digital-first luxury brand specializing in high-end lab-grown diamonds, targeting the North American market and the South Asian/Middle Eastern diaspora.

**Tagline:** Modern Brilliance. Ancient Soul.

## Business Model — "Lean Luxury"
- Made-to-Order experience (no finished inventory held)
- Strong relationships with diamond manufacturers
- Robust custom-jewelry production network
- Customers select stones and settings to fit their vision and budget
- Settings available in 9ct/14ct gold

## Target Audience
- North American luxury buyers
- South Asian / Middle Eastern diaspora

## Phase 1 — Marketing Landing Site (current)
A sophisticated, trust-focused marketing site that communicates the brand philosophy and captures leads. Plain HTML/CSS, designed to eventually migrate to Shopify.

## Phase 2 — Full E-Commerce Platform (future, Shopify)
1. **Live Diamond Feed Integration** — real-time global inventory via Nivoda, VDB, or IDEX API
2. **Custom Ring Builder** — pair live stones with manufacturing-ready settings
3. **Digital Concierge Tools** — virtual consultations, 3D CAD visualization previews
4. **Trust-Focused Design** — sophisticated aesthetic aligned with brand philosophy

## Design Direction
- High-end design studio feel, not a standard store
- Sophisticated aesthetic
- Communicates "Modern Brilliance. Ancient Soul." philosophy
- Builds trust with luxury buyers

## Current Status
Sourcing and manufacturing pipeline secured. Ready to build web presence.
